# Learn tab sources

The Learn content (Golden rules, the 8 topic cards, Report a scam) lives in `lib/l10n/app_en.arb` / `app_fil.arb` under the `learn*` keys. It is summarised in plain words from these public Philippine advisories. The sample texts are made up for teaching. Their brands and domains show what scams look like, and none of them is a real message or site. Check these before changing the copy, and recheck the official numbers and sites before each release.

| Topic | Sources |
|---|---|
| Golden rules, fake bank and e-wallet texts | BSP #BSPSecuriTips consumer advisories (never share your OTP, PIN or password; contact your bank only through official channels), bsp.gov.ph |
| Fake prizes and raffles | DTI sales promotion permit rules (a legitimate raffle carries a DTI permit number), dti.gov.ph; CICC scam advisories |
| Family emergency texts | PNP-ACG and CICC advisories on impostor and "new number" money requests |
| Fake government aid (ayuda) | DSWD, SSS and PhilHealth advisories against fake payout links; CICC advisories |
| Fake delivery and parcel fees | CICC and DICT advisories on parcel and delivery fee text scams |
| Fake jobs and online tasks | CICC and PNP-ACG advisories on "task" and like-for-pay job scams |
| Investment and crypto scams | SEC investment scam advisories (check that a firm may take investments), sec.gov.ph |
| Online gambling ads | PAGCOR and CICC warnings on illegal online gambling sites; NTC orders on gambling text spam, ntc.gov.ph |
| Report a scam | CICC Inter-Agency Response Center hotline 1326 (toll-free, 24/7; CICC and DICT); eGov PH app Report > Scam > Text Scam (CICC and DICT); BSP Online Buddy (BOB) at bsp.gov.ph, after first contacting your bank or e-wallet; PNP Anti-Cybercrime Group, acg.pnp.gov.ph; NTC complaints, ntc.gov.ph |

Coverage that confirmed the 1326 hotline and the eGov app reporting flow: Manila Bulletin, 19 Jul 2023 and 3 Jul 2024; Inquirer Technology, 2024; Philstar, 21 Mar 2025. Coverage that confirmed BOB: Inquirer Business, 2020; BSP advisories republished by member banks, 2025-2026.

The Filipino copy still needs review by a native speaker, as all safety copy does (see CLAUDE.md).

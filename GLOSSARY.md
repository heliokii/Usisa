# Glossary

**Check**: The action a person takes to ask Usisa about something suspicious. It starts from one of four home tiles (Scan a message, Check a link, Scan a screenshot, AI scan) or from sharing into Usisa. Whichever way it starts, it ends in one Verdict.

**AI scan**: The one kind of Check that also asks the AI. The other three tiles judge with the phone's own check plus the online link check only. A person reaches AI scan from its tile, or from "Ask AI to look closer" on a Be careful or Couldn't check Verdict.

**Verdict**: The single answer a Check gives. It is always exactly one of:
- **Looks safe**: nothing suspicious was found. Never a guarantee.
- **Be careful**: some warning signs; don't act until you're sure.
- **Dangerous**: strong scam signs; don't tap, reply or send money.
- **Couldn't check**: Usisa could not finish examining it (offline, or a service failed). Never shown or worded as safe.

**Warning sign**: A specific thing in a message or link that points to a Scam, shown to the person as a reason. A **strong sign** (a request for an OTP or password together with a link, a fake look-alike of a known brand's domain, a flagged link) can raise the Verdict. A **weak sign** (urgent wording, a brand name mentioned, a money request between family) is only shown as a reason and never changes the Verdict by itself.

**Incoming SMS check**: The automatic Check Usisa runs on its own when a text message arrives, without the person starting it. It only raises a notification when the Verdict is Be careful or Dangerous.

**Scam**: A message or link trying to take money, account access or personal information. Only scams earn Be careful or Dangerous.

**Harmless promo**: Unwanted but harmless advertising (sales, load promos). It is "spam" in the everyday sense but not a Scam, so its Verdict is Looks safe.
_Avoid_: using "spam" to mean Scam.

**Gambling ad**: An unsolicited message promoting betting, casino, slots, e-sabong or bonus credits, whatever brand it names. Neither a Scam nor a Harmless promo: its Verdict is Be careful, or Dangerous when it also carries Scam signs.
_Avoid_: lumping it in with Harmless promo. A "you won, claim your prize" text is a Scam, not a Gambling ad; a plain lotto result with no link or request is Looks safe.

**Real destination**: Where a link actually ends up after following all its redirects, for example where a short link like `bit.ly/...` really leads. It is shown to the person in place of the short form.
